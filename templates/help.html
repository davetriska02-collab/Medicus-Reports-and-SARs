Help

Workflow

SAR Redact follows a five-step process. First, upload the patient's medical record (PDF, TIF, ZIP, RTF, or TXT). SAR Redact automatically detects personally identifiable information and classifies each instance. In the review screen, a GP approves, rejects, or excludes each candidate. An admin signs off the reviewed redactions and clicks Finalise. The system then generates the redacted PDF output, ready for disclosure.

Redaction categories

Person name — patient's full name, first name, last name, and configured aliases. NHS number — 10-digit NHS identifier. Address — residential address and postcode. Date of birth — any date matching the subject's DOB. Phone — phone numbers. Email — email addresses. Safeguarding — high-sensitivity terms flagged for extra scrutiny. Custom word — phrases added in Settings.

File formats

SAR Redact accepts PDF, TIF/TIFF, RTF, TXT, PNG, JPG, HTML, CDAX (GP2GP clinical documents), and ZIP archives containing any of the above. All formats are converted to PDF internally before analysis. Scanned PDFs may have lower detection accuracy than text-layer PDFs.
